Fast cash after a wipe

Do these in order. Each step pays for the next.

The plan

  1. Rent a plot

    The Contraband Workbench has to sit on your own base. Game data

    Spent so far: about $150,000

  2. Build the Contraband Workbench and start repacking

    Cheap to start, and it repacks while you do runs. Players net $10k to $20k a crate. Player report

    Spent so far: about $190,000

  3. Open a bank account and launder every sale

    Banked money can't be stolen by players. Do it after every run, not at the end of the night. Kefir

    Spent so far: about $190,000

  4. Add a Label Printer

    Faster repacks mean more batches per night. Game data

    Spent so far: about $235,000

  5. Add a Tool Box

    A second crate per batch roughly doubles what one bench makes. Game data

    Spent so far: about $585,000

  6. Fuse the building that holds your cargo, then a Personal Alarm

    Only once cargo sits there long enough to be worth raiding. Clans: Clan Alarm next. Zoi tip

    Spent so far: about $860,000

  7. Upgrade to a Small House and add a second bench

    A Trailer fits one workbench; a Small House fits two. Player report

    Spent so far: about $1,700,000

Already own a Grow Room? Filling its tables beats the bench. See Best runs. Player report

Tips

  • Keep the bench busy. Load crates before every run so it never sits empty. Zoi tip
  • Buy Contraband Cargo from your settlement's seller. Residents get a discount. Kefir
  • Sell cargo in one trip with your crew. Police can seize it on the road. Game data
  • Never leave cash or cargo at the base or in the trunk. Player report
  • If you're close to the next Notoriety level, wait to sell big cargo. Level 5 pays 25% more than level 1. Game data

Last day

  • Don't start long builds or research. They won't pay back before the raid. Game data
  • Sell all cargo. Every $500,000 you hold at the wipe is one more year of jail time, up to 40 years. Game data
  • Banked cash is wiped too, but it still counts toward jail time. Kefir
  • Agree with your crew on which realm you all join next. Game data

Best runs

Cash per hour, ranked. All of these are player reports, one player's or squad's result, not a promise. Player report

  1. Full Grow Room~$200k / hour

    6 tables, 36 bushes. Grows while you rob or are offline, and a death doesn't stop it. One table of 6 bushes is ~$34k / hour.

  2. Store runs~$200k / hour

    One solo player made $600k in 3 hours.

  3. Cop shiftup to ~$130k / hour

    Best report: $100k in 45 min. A normal shift is closer to $30k. You keep your own gear.

  4. Contraband bench~$20k / hour per bench

    $10k to $20k net per 30-minute crate. Passive, so stack it with everything above.

Per job, not per hour

  • Bank heist$1.5M to $5.9M

    Squad job. Needs a Drill, an ID Card and a Mini Drill, and the drill alerts the whole city.

  • ATM restock event~$150k

    How often it happens isn't known.

Make each run pay more

  • Pick stores by safes per minute, not size. A jewelry store with two safes beats a hotel with five cabinets. Player report
  • Sell at your district's hub when you can. Kefir says it can pay well above Looty Bay. Kefir
  • Plant bushes instead of selling them. A planted bush keeps paying every harvest. Late in the week, sell it. Player report
  • Leave at about $100k in the bag. Smaller cash-outs lose less when a squad shows up. Player report

Unknown: Nobody has published reliable per-minute numbers for specific crates or routes, how often a repack comes out as Rarity Cargo, or what the Nov 4 patch did to production payouts.

Duo playbook

Two players, one Clan Alarm. Split the jobs so one of you is always free.

On a run

  • Driver: stays in the car, engine ready, hands empty. Drawing a weapon drops cargo you're carrying. Player report
  • Guard: does the store. Break the alarm box and shoot the cameras first, and skip the glass. Player report
  • Loot and sell in separate trips, so you rarely lose both. Player report
  • Before selling, one of you checks the Fence or Smuggler as a Civilian. Campers wait there. Player report

When the Clan Alarm fires

  • It warns both of you the moment a hack starts. Game data
  • Whoever is closer goes home. The other one finishes the sale or banks, so the run isn't lost too. Zoi tip
  • You have time: each decoder takes about 12 seconds at the panel, so a fused building takes minutes. Player report
  • After a hack, repair the panel on the spot. Your fuses survive and the defense comes back. Player report

Banking

  • Both of you launder after every sale. Banked money can't be stolen by players. Kefir
  • Use your own Laundering Desk; Lenny takes a share. Player report
  • Withdraw only what the next buy needs. Cash on you can be taken. Zoi tip

Raid defense

Nothing protects an offline base except fuses and alarms. Here's how raiders get in and what to do about it.

How raiders get in

  • They hack the gate panel from outside, then a building's Security System with decoders until its defense hits zero. Then that building opens for a while. Player report
  • One hack opens one building, not the whole base. Player report
  • They go for the weakest money buildings first: the Grow Room and Chemical Laboratory have some of the lowest base defense. Player report
  • Decoders don't open safes or cage racks. That takes a Master Key. Game data
  • An open door lets anyone who jumps the fence walk straight in. Player report
  • Eviction Rights were pulled from barter on Nov 17, for now. Kefir

Where to keep cargo

  • Best: nowhere. Sell it and bank the cash. Zoi tip
  • If it has to wait, put it in a cage rack or safe, so a raider also needs a Master Key. Game data
  • Spread cash, loot and cargo over several buildings. Each one needs its own decoders, so raiders run out. Player report
  • Not in the car. A trunk can reportedly be opened anywhere. Player report

Fuse and alarm order

  • Fuse the Grow Room and Chemical Laboratory first. They make the money and open the easiest. Player report
  • Fill slots with Fuse I first. It's the cheapest per point; move to bigger fuses when slots run out. Game data
  • Put your Clan Alarm on the building that holds the most value. A panel has 3 alarm slots. Player report
  • Alarms add no defense. They only warn you. Game data
  • Close every door before you log off. Player report
