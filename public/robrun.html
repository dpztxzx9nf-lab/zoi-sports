Best buy order after a wipe

For a duo that likes gunfights. The Grow Room pays in the background while you fight. Buy in this order and keep $100k cash on hand.

PlanLandBest runsDuoRaids

The buy order

The Grow Room is the core of this plan. Fill all 6 tables and put a Fan, Water Pump and UV Lamp on each as soon as your plot level allows. One player reports over $500k from about 40 minutes of harvesting with upgraded tables. Player report

Pays back is in hours you're online. The short end is math from game prices, the long end is from player reports.

  1. Starter kit for both: Harris 500 shotgun, Light Armor, Lockpick, Sports Bag, meds and plates, plus one Action Camera

    Store fights happen inside 10 m, where a $3,500 shotgun downs a player in two shells, as fast as a $115k SMG. Keep the junk you loot: it packs into collection crates later. Game data

    Costs $95,700 · total $95,700 · pays back: first store fight

  2. Land Plot Title I: rent a level 1 plot next to a Reservoir

    Pick a hex next to a Reservoir: each harvest gets a 50% chance of extra bags. Your base, own car and laundering all need a plot. Player report

    Costs $150,000 · total $245,700 · pays back: unlocks the base

  3. Grow Room + 1 Grow Table

    Bushes can't be bought. Plant every one you find; they regrow after each harvest. Player report

    Costs $320,000 · total $565,700 · pays back: 4 to 9 h

  4. Fight kit I: KL-545 rifle and Medium Armor for both, Pouch II, Flash Grenades, First Aid Cabinet

    The KL-545 is the best-value rifle for anything outside a store. Flashes break contact. The cabinet lets you heal and respawn at base when someone raids you. Game data

    Costs $139,400 · total $705,100 · pays back: first fight you win

  5. Grow Tables 2 to 6

    Add one each time you have 6 more bushes. Best payback in the game. Player report

    Costs $200,000 · total $905,100 · pays back: under 1.5 h each

  6. Fan on all 6 tables

    Bushes grow 12% faster. Do this before anything that isn't the Grow Room. Game data

    Costs $250,000 · total $1,155,100 · pays back: 4 to 9 h

  7. Bank account, clan, and a claim on a small business

    Clan wars are the best-paying fight: a small business is a 3v3, the win pays $250k, and one Pharmacy panel showed +$83,333 an hour after that. You need a third player, fights run 8pm to 6am, and dying loses your items. Game data

    Costs $55,000 · total $1,210,100 · pays back: one win

  8. Collection Table

    Packs a few cheap stolen items into a $50k crate. Sets are worth far more than the same items sold to the Fence. Game data

    Costs $20,000 · total $1,230,100 · pays back: first set

  9. Contraband Workbench in the Trailer

    Repack $10k crates into $35k ones. Players net $10k to $20k a crate. Player report

    Costs $40,000 · total $1,270,100 · pays back: 1 to 2 h

  10. Longhorn Van + Trunk Bags

    6 cargo slots, 12 with racks. Every other early car has 1. Game data

    Costs $251,000 · total $1,521,100 · pays back: hauling

  11. Car Lift + Workshop + 4 Contraband Workbenches

    The Workshop fits 4 benches. A Small House adds only 1 more, for $800k. Player report

    Costs $295,000 · total $1,816,100 · pays back: 1.5 to 4 h

  12. Label Printer on all 5 benches

    Repacks run 15% faster. Game data

    Costs $165,000 · total $1,981,100 · pays back: 4 to 11 h

  13. Additional Collection

    7 more sets for the Collection Table. Game data

    Costs $59,000 · total $2,040,100 · pays back: first set

  14. Land Plot Title II: move to a level 2 plot

    Opens the Mini Drill, Water Pump, rifles and armor that match tier II players, Special Collection and Titan. Game data

    Costs $1,000,000 · total $3,040,100 · pays back: unlocks tier II

  15. 3 Mini Drills + Master Key

    Mini Drills open the big safes in locked rooms, which drop decoders. One player got 25 decoders in two runs, enough to open a gate and a Grow Room with green fuses. The Master Key opens safes on other bases. Player report

    Costs $259,000 · total $3,299,100 · pays back: first raided Grow Room

  16. Fight kit II: RC 5.56 and Assault Armor for both, Injectors

    By now other crews wear tier II armor. The RC 5.56 keeps its damage out to 60 m; Injectors heal fast mid-fight. Game data

    Costs $699,000 · total $3,998,100 · pays back: fights vs tier II crews

  17. Water Pump on all 6 tables

    Another 14% off grow time. It's the main reason to go level 2. Game data

    Costs $620,000 · total $4,618,100 · pays back: 7 to 14 h

  18. Special Collection

    Sets that pack into $150k crates. Game data

    Costs $150,000 · total $4,768,100 · pays back: one crate

  19. Longhorn Titan (optional)

    Almost twice the Van's speed, same 6 cargo slots. Sells back for $360k. Game data

    Costs $1,100,000 · total $5,868,100 · pays back: faster runs

  20. Land Plot Title III: move to a level 3 plot

    Go here if you'll run the bank or have 20+ hours of play left. Otherwise stop at step 19 and bank the rest. Opens the bank's ID Card, the SR Reaper, Heavy Armor and UV Lamps. Game data

    Costs $3,000,000 · total $8,868,100 · pays back: unlocks tier III

  21. Bank kit: Deposit Box Keys, Code Tablet, 2 USB Hacking Tools, purple Action Camera, $50k for the Drill barter

    Fill the keys at the bank's deposit boxes and trade them for the ID Card. The camera, USBs and cash trade for the Drill at Lenny's. Bring the 3 Mini Drills from step 15. The vault drill may need a minimum number of players online; that isn't confirmed. Player report

    Costs $510,000 · total $9,378,100 · pays back: one bank job: $1.5M to $5.9M

  22. UV Lamp on all 6 tables

    Bushes grow 24% faster. With all three upgrades a table grows twice as fast. Game data

    Costs $2,000,000 · total $11,378,100 · pays back: 7 to 15 h

  23. Unique Collection

    Sets that pack into $500k crates. Game data

    Costs $350,000 · total $11,728,100 · pays back: one crate

  24. Fight kit III: SR Reaper and Heavy Armor for both

    The fastest gun through Heavy armor at any range. Only while you keep winning fights: a gun is lost when you die, armor can be repaired with plates. Game data

    Costs $1,420,000 · total $13,148,100 · pays back: best gear in the game

Cars

  • Longhorn Van first, Titan later. Both carry 6 crates; the Titan is much faster. Game data
  • Skip the Royal E and J, the Longhorn Wild and the Buletti. They cost $1M to $10M and carry 0 to 4 crates. Game data

Rent or own

  • Level 2 and 3: rent. Owning a level 2 settlement costs about $1.55M more. At the 5% laundering tax you'd have to launder about $31M to win that back. Game data
  • Level 3: rent. Owning costs $8M and won't pay back within one wipe. Game data
  • Level 1: own if a free settlement is close in the first 3 days. It's $300k vs $150k. You skip the 5% tax and collect it from tenants, so it pays back in about 3 days if you launder $1M a day. Game data
  • On a brand-new server you may have to own: you can only rent where someone already owns the settlement. Game data

Grow Room or Workshop

  • Fill the Grow Room's 6 tables first. If your plot only fits two buildings, take a Grow Room and a Workshop, not two Grow Rooms: bushes can't be bought, so a second room may sit empty. Zoi tip

Tips

  • Keep every bench loaded. Reload them every 30 minutes. Zoi tip
  • Buy Contraband Cargo from your settlement's seller. Residents get a discount. Kefir
  • Sell cargo in one trip with your crew. Police can seize it on the road. Game data
  • Never leave cash or cargo at the base or in the trunk. Player report
  • If you're close to the next Notoriety level, wait to sell big cargo. Level 5 pays 25% more than level 1. Game data

Last day

  • Don't start long builds or research. They won't pay back before the raid. Game data
  • Last grow cycle: harvest, then sell each bush to the Smuggler. A bush is worth $20k or more. Game data
  • Sell all cargo. Every $500,000 you hold at the wipe is one more year of jail time, up to 40 years. Game data
  • Banked cash is wiped too, but it still counts toward jail time. Kefir
  • Agree with your crew on which realm you all join next. Game data

Best runs

Cash per hour, ranked. All of these are player reports, one player's or squad's result, not a promise. Player report

  1. Full Grow Room~$200k / hour

    6 tables, 36 bushes. Grows while you rob or are offline, and a death doesn't stop it. One table of 6 bushes is ~$34k / hour.

  2. Store runs~$200k / hour

    One solo player made $600k in 3 hours.

  3. Cop shiftup to ~$130k / hour

    Best report: $100k in 45 min. A normal shift is closer to $30k. Pay caps at $1M a shift. You keep your own gear.

  4. Contraband bench~$20k / hour per bench

    $10k to $20k net per 30-minute crate. Passive, so stack it with everything above.

Per job, not per hour

  • Bank heist$1.5M to $5.9M

    Squad job, tier III. Needs 3 Mini Drills, the Drill and the ID Card (step 21). A cop pulls the vault drill in 4 seconds, so one of you guards it.

  • Small business (clan war)$250k + ~$83k / hour

    3v3 for a $50k bet. The hourly figure is one Pharmacy panel.

  • ATM tip-off~$150k

    How often it happens isn't known.

Make each run pay more

  • Pick stores by safes per minute, not size. A jewelry store with two safes beats a hotel with five cabinets. Player report
  • Sell at your district's hub when you can. Kefir says it can pay well above Looty Bay. Kefir
  • Plant bushes instead of selling them. A planted bush keeps paying every harvest. Late in the week, sell it. Player report
  • Leave at about $100k in the bag. Smaller cash-outs lose less when a squad shows up. Player report

Unknown: Nobody has published reliable per-minute numbers for specific crates or routes, how often a repack comes out as Rarity Cargo, or what the Nov 4 patch did to production payouts.

Duo playbook

Two players, one base. Fight together. Whoever is closer to base after a fight reloads the Grow Room.

Who does what

  • Both: stores, heists and fights, together. Kefir says solo players struggle against full parties. Kefir
  • Between fights, one of you harvests, replants, reloads the benches and launders. The Grow Room keeps growing while you fight. Zoi tip
  • One of you does all the research and buying, so nothing is paid for twice. Hand gear over. Zoi tip

In a fight

  • Shotgun inside stores, rifle outside. A shotgun is the best gun inside 10 m and a bad one across a street. Game data
  • Shoot the alarm box and cameras first, and hold the clerk at gunpoint instead of killing them. Player report
  • Whoever drives keeps the gun holstered. Drawing a weapon drops cargo you're carrying. Player report
  • Don't shoot civilians. 3 kills marks you as Most Wanted and shows your location to the whole city. Game data
  • Cornered by cops? Surrender: you lose the bag, not your gear. You can also offer a bribe from your bank, up to $200k. Game data

When to split

  • Notoriety rises with each crime in one visit and resets when you leave. A duo starts at level 2 (×1.10), solo at level 4 (×1.35), and level 5 pays ×1.50. Game data
  • After a spree together, sell together before you leave. At level 4 or 5 you match or beat the solo bonus, with two guns at the Fence. Zoi tip
  • Harvest bags and loot from earlier visits: one of you sells alone for +35% ($40k became $54k). Small batches. The other covers from a car nearby or reloads the base. Player report

Fights worth taking

  • Clan wars for a business (step 7). Best pay of any fight, but a small business is 3v3, so bring a third player. Game data
  • ATM tip-off: the city alert marks one ATM with a big cash batch, and the first crew to open it wins. No tool needed. Drop what you're doing unless you're carrying cargo. Game data
  • Raiding bases: pick a Grow Room with green fuses, not in your own district. One player got his full Grow Room this way, and bushes can't be bought. Player report
  • Sellers at a Fence or Smuggler: Kefir says camping there is intended. One seller says he brings $100k to $200k a trip, up to $500k when he risks it. Only hit a loaded car, and expect cops and other crews. Player report
  • Outside the city anyone can be killed. Crews camp the roads out for cars carrying bank loot. Player report

Fights to skip

  • Cop shifts are fight-heavy but pay little: $9k to $15k an arrest, $2k a kill, and nothing for killing someone who surrendered. Max 4 cops per city. Worth it only when you're broke, because you use their gear, not yours. Game data
  • A second robbery with a full bag. That's where most players lose the first one. Player report

When the Clan Alarm fires

  • It warns both of you the moment a hack starts. Game data
  • Whoever is closer goes home. The other one finishes the sale or banks, so the run isn't lost too. Zoi tip
  • You have time: each decoder takes about 12 seconds at the panel, so a fused building takes minutes. Player report
  • After a hack, repair the panel on the spot. Your fuses survive and the defense comes back. Player report

Banking

  • Both of you launder after every sale. Banked money can't be stolen by players. Kefir
  • Use your own Laundering Desk; Lenny takes a share. Player report
  • Withdraw only what the next buy needs. Cash on you can be taken. Zoi tip

Raid defense

Nothing protects an offline base except fuses and alarms. Here's how raiders get in and what to do about it.

How raiders get in

  • They hack the gate panel from outside, then a building's Security System with decoders until its defense hits zero. Then that building opens for a while. Player report
  • One hack opens one building, not the whole base. Player report
  • They go for the weakest money buildings first: the Grow Room and Chemical Laboratory have some of the lowest base defense. Player report
  • Decoders don't open safes or cage racks. That takes a Master Key. Game data
  • An open door lets anyone who jumps the fence walk straight in. Player report
  • Eviction Rights were pulled from barter on Nov 17, for now. Kefir

Where to keep cargo

  • Best: nowhere. Sell it and bank the cash. Zoi tip
  • If it has to wait, put it in a cage rack or safe, so a raider also needs a Master Key. Game data
  • Spread cash, loot and cargo over several buildings. Each one needs its own decoders, so raiders run out. Player report
  • Not in the car. A trunk can reportedly be opened anywhere. Player report

Fuse and alarm order

  • Fuse the Grow Room first, then the Workshop. They hold your income, and the Grow Room opens easily. Zoi tip
  • Fill slots with Fuse I first. It's the cheapest per point; move to bigger fuses when slots run out. Game data
  • Put your Clan Alarm on the building that holds the most value. A panel has 3 alarm slots. Player report
  • Alarms add no defense. They only warn you. Game data
  • Close every door before you log off. Player report
