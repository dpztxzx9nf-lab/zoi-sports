Kept vs lost

Kept

  • Cosmetics
  • Appearance and name
  • B-Coins
  • Settings
  • Tutorial progress
  • Jail time (Authority)

Lost

  • Plots and bases
  • Weapons and gear
  • Cars
  • Loot and cargo
  • Settlements
  • Research and shipment unlocks
  • Cash, banked cash too

Official Everything lost is turned into jail time and Authority. Clan membership: one fan wiki lists it as kept, Kefir has not said. Surrendering to change servers drops you from your clan.

Cash to jail time

Game data 1 year per $500,000 of property, up to 40 years ($20,000,000) per wipe. Paying Lenny's $1,000,000 debt adds 10 years once.

Last day

  1. Don't start long builds or research.
  2. Sell your cargo; sold cash is a sure number.
  3. Agree with your clan which realm you all join next.
