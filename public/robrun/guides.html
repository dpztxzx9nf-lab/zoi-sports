Stack and bank

  1. Launder after every run. Official Money in the bank can't be stolen by players until the wipe.
  2. Keep nothing at the base. Cash and cargo there can be raided.
  3. Sell before the wipe. Each $500,000 you hold then is +1 year of jail time, up to $20,000,000.
  4. Skip big buys on the last day. They won't pay back before the raid.

Official Banked cash is wiped too, but it still counts toward your jail time and Authority.
