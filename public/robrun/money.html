The loop

  1. Buy Contraband Cargo
  2. Repack at the Contraband Workbench
  3. Sell to the Smuggler's Van
  4. Launder into the bank

Game data Contraband Cargo goes in, Premium Cargo (or Rarity Cargo) comes out. What decides Rarity isn't confirmed.

Calculator

Game data Buy: smuggling seller $10,000, settlement seller $5,000. Sell: Premium Cargo $35,000, Rarity Cargo $40,000, plus your Notoriety bonus.

Player report About 30 minutes per repack with no upgrades. Label Printers make it faster, so lower the minutes if yours is quicker. Your own Laundering Desk takes no cut; Lenny takes a share, and a settlement owner taxes renters. Police can seize cargo on the way (about $4,000 per raw crate).
