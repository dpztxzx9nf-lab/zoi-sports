In-game

Zoi tip Mid laptops (RTX 4060 class, high-refresh screen).

SettingSet toWhy
Anti-aliasingSMAACheap, keeps far players readable
ShadowsLowBig FPS and heat cost
GrassLowHides nothing useful when low
WaterLowBig cost, no gain
HBAOOffCostly ambient shading
Depth of fieldOffBlurs distant targets
Motion blurOffSmears tracking
VSyncOffAdds input delay
Render scale1.0Lower blurs the image
Frame capSet oneHighest option your game holds in busy fights

Outside the game

  • Mouse polling rate 500 Hz or less if FPS drops when you move the mouse
  • Turn off Discord and GeForce overlays
  • Install on an SSD

Official Minimum: i5-6600K or Ryzen 5 1600, 12 GB RAM, GTX 1050 Ti or RX 580. Recommended: i5-9600K or Ryzen 5 3600, 16 GB RAM, RTX 2070 or RX 5700 XT.
