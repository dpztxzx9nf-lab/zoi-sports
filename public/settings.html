The scope multiplier is the odd one out

A magnified optic needs less stick movement for the same result, not more, so 1.12 usually means it was never adjusted. Worth testing below 1.0.

Shadows at 0 also means losing the shadow that tells you someone's coming around a corner. That matters more in Zero Build, where cover is buildings and terrain.

SettingValue
Headphone modeOn

In Zero Build there are no structures to muffle footsteps and reloads, so audio carries further. That makes this the highest-value corner of the settings for hearing people first.

What to change, in order

  1. Fix the scope multiplier first. Take 1.12 below 1.0. One setting, most likely to be an accident, smallest thing to test.
  2. Then raise ADS look speed, a few points at a time. From 12 upward, giving each step a real session before the next.
  3. Then put the paddles on weapon slots. Hardware-side, so it doesn't touch the game's settings. Do it after the aim change settles.
  4. Then leave it alone for two weeks. A sensitivity change takes days to stop feeling wrong and weeks to show in results.
