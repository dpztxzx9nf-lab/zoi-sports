The Wolverine's extra buttons don't appear in Fortnite's config at all, because the controller remaps in hardware, so whether they're in use isn't visible here. In Zero Build, with no building eating buttons, direct weapon-slot selection on those paddles is the clearest thing left on the table.

What to test, in order

  1. Start at the scope multiplier. 1.12 makes scoped turns slightly quicker than aimed ones. Test a step up or down from it, one value at a time, and judge it over a full session.
  2. Then try a small move in ADS look speed. From 12, a few points either way, giving each step a real session before the next.
  3. Then put the paddles on weapon slots. Hardware-side, so it doesn't touch the game's settings. Do it after the aim change settles.
  4. Then leave it alone for two weeks. Any aim change takes days to stop feeling off and weeks to show up in results.
