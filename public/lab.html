        How to read this

        A setting nudge is small next to the noise of a few nights, so the verdicts run on kills a match, which moves fast enough to read, and win rate is the slow second opinion underneath it. The window is counted in games rather than days: at about five kills a match, a one-kill move needs roughly 190 games a side to prove out, and a week can hold twelve games or two hundred.
