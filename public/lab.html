What this can tell you

A setting nudge is small next to the noise of a few nights of Fortnite. Win rate is the worst of it: at around eleven percent, a hundred games can only pin it down to within about six points either way, so a two-point move is not a finding, it is the same number twice. Settling a three-point win-rate move would take roughly one thousand seven hundred games on each side. That is months of play for one setting, and it is not going to happen.

Kills a match moves fast enough to read. One kill a match of change settles in about one hundred and ninety games a side at a lifetime rate near five, so that is what the verdicts go on now, and win rate sits underneath as a slow second opinion that never decides on its own. A setting worth testing is one where you expect about a kill a match to move. Anything finer than that is a feeling, and a feeling is fine to act on, as long as the lab is not pretending it measured it.
