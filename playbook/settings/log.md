# In-game settings log

Every change to Fortnite's own settings, newest first. Graphics, display, input and audio all go here. Hardware and the PC itself do not — those live with Knexrig.

## How to read a row

Same shape as the [sensitivity log](../sensitivity/log.md): **date**, **what changed** (field, old → new), **why**, **verdict**, and **what changed in aim or play**.

The last column earns its place even for settings that look purely visual. Turning shadows off is a frame-rate choice, but it also means losing the shadow that tells you someone's about to walk around a corner. Log that, not just the setting.

## Not logged here

Anything that's really about the machine: frame-time traces, drivers, overclocks, capture settings, monitor calibration. That's Knexrig's lane, and mixing it in here makes both logs harder to read.
