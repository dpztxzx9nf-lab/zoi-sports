## How to write an aim note

Observations, not judgements. "Shot high on the second and third shot of a burst" is useful. "Aim was bad today" isn't.

Where a note suggests a cause, say so as a guess and keep it short — sensitivity drift, a keybind that's awkward to reach, a crosshair that disappears against certain backgrounds, a night of bad sleep. The guess is what makes the next change testable.

## What doesn't go here

Scores that come from the machine rather than the player — frame drops, stutter, input lag from a background process. Those go to Knexrig, and if they're making aim look bad, that's important to say out loud rather than logging it as an aim problem here.
