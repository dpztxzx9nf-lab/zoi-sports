# Strategy — Zero Build

How Zoi plays the game. Zero Build specifically: no walls, no edits, no build fights. Cover is whatever the map already has, and that changes almost every decision.

## The four decisions

Every Zero Build game comes down to these, in order. Worth writing down what Zoi does for each rather than playing it by feel every match.

### 1. Drop

Where, and why there. Distance from the bus line, how contested it usually is, what loot it offers, and whether the rotation out is survivable on foot.

### 2. Loadout

Slot priority and what gets left behind. Zero Build puts real weight on mobility and heals, since there's no wall to hide behind while you heal.

### 3. Rotation

Getting from the drop to the next circle with cover the whole way. The interesting question is what to do when there isn't any — cross open ground early, or hold and take the risk later.

### 4. Endgame

Last few circles with no builds. Positioning relative to high ground and hard cover matters more than in build mode, and third parties are the main threat.
